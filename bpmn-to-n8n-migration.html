02 / Mapping

BPMN functionality maps to CHWF/n8n patterns

The aim is not to reproduce BPMN syntax. It is to preserve the required business outcomes using supported platform patterns.

BPMN capabilities and corresponding CHWF and n8n patterns
BPMN capabilityCHWF/n8n patternBusiness outcome
Process startsTriggers and webhooksStart work from a person, system or schedule
User tasksWIL and wait/resumeAssign secure actions, forms and approvals

Core proposition: equivalent outcomes matter more than equivalent notation.

05 / Recommendation
Recommendation

Complete the proof
before expanding the platform

  • Continue validating CHWF/n8n against representative BPMN-style workflows.
  • Complete proof through real migration scenarios, including the difficult cases.
  • Introduce another workflow engine only if material capability gaps are demonstrated.

Immediate next step: agree the validation criteria, complete the FOI evidence, and test the unresolved scenarios before making a platform decision.
